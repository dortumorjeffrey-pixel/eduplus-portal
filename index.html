Caution — Things You Cannot Do

Read these rules before entering the portal. They protect every student and lecturer.

These are hard rules enforced by the app. If you attempt any of them, the action will be blocked with an explanation.

Course Registration (Lecturers)

  • You cannot register two active courses with the same course code — not even if another lecturer owns the code. One active course per code.
  • You cannot register a code that another lecturer is still using. The code becomes free only after that course is unregistered.
  • Re-registering the same code + same title after it was closed is allowed, but it becomes a separate offering (Offering 2, 3, …) with completely separate content.
  • The same code with a different title is an entirely different course, not a continuation.
  • The same title under a different code is also an entirely different course.
  • You cannot bring old assignments, announcements, marks or past questions into a new offering — no content carries over.

Course Access (Students)

  • You cannot join a course code that has no active course. If the lecturer unregistered it, the code cannot deliver new information.
  • You cannot join the same course twice. Joining a newer offering replaces your closed card instead of stacking duplicates.
  • You cannot receive content from a closed course. Its card moves to Closed Courses — Unregistered and shows no new assignments, past questions, marks or announcements.
  • You cannot see another offering's content under the same course code. Every offering is isolated.
  • You cannot submit assignments without registering your ID/Index number, and you cannot submit after an assignment's deadline.
  • You cannot send a live classroom answer without selecting a course first, and attachments above 1.5MB are rejected.

Deletion & Data Rules

  • When a student permanently deletes a past question or information item, it is removed from that student's account only. Other students keep their copy and shared course content is never deleted.
  • A permanently deleted item cannot be restored by that student. Only temporarily dismissed items appear under Restore Information.
  • A lecturer unregistering a course does not delete shared content. The course is closed for students, and the code becomes free for a future offering.
  • Deleting a lecturer account does not delete the courses' shared content either. The courses are automatically unregistered and students see the closed-course notice.
  • A closed course cannot be re-opened. Registering again creates a new offering with new content.

Accounts & Subscriptions

  • You cannot register more than one account with the same email — whether student or lecturer. Use a different email for a different account.
  • You cannot bypass the monthly subscription. Student access is Ghc 20/month and lecturer access is Ghc 50/month via Paystack / PayPass.
  • You cannot recover a permanently purged account after its 30-day deletion window has passed.
  • You cannot use course features while offline — joining, posting, grading and account changes require an internet connection.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Payment, Live Answers, and account guides for Lecturers and Students

What is the Live Classroom Quick Answer feature?
When the lecturer asks a verbal question in class, students who feel shy to speak aloud can submit their answers instantly through the Live Classroom Quick Answer section. The lecturer receives it in real-time on their dashboard with the student's name, ID, and profile picture.
How do I type in another language (Twi, Ewe, French, Arabic, Russian, Hindi, etc.)?
Click the globe "Language" button at the top of the page. A small keyboard panel opens at the bottom of the screen — you can drag it by its top bar, minimise it, or close it so it never hides what you are typing. Choose your language from the list (including Twi, Ewe, Ga, Dagbani, Yorùbá, Igbo, Hausa, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Chinese Pinyin, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Swahili and Latin), then either click the keys in the panel or switch on "Use my laptop keyboard" so your physical keys type that language. While the laptop keyboard is mapped, a small key guide stays on screen showing exactly which letter each key types — close it with the × and reopen it from the small "keys" button if you need more space. To keep the page readable, the keyboard and the key guide fade to a lighter, slightly smaller view after about 10 seconds without use; move your mouse over them (or press a key) and they come back fully. The half-circle button in the keyboard header, and the matching button in the key guide header, make them transparent straight away and keep them that way until you press it again. Your choice is saved on that device, and you can switch back to English at any time.
How much does registration & monthly subscription cost?
Student registration and monthly subscription is Ghc 20. Lecturer registration and monthly subscription is Ghc 50. Each subscription gives full access to the portal for 1 month (30 days).
What is PayPass and how does it work?
PayPass allows you to recharge/resubscribe anytime even before your current subscription ends! When you use PayPass, another full 30 days are automatically added to your remaining balance. For example, if you have 5 days left, PayPass extends it to 1 month and 5 days (35 days total).
What happens if my subscription expires?
When your subscription expires, your account is temporarily locked with a PayPass renewal screen until you recharge. Once recharged, your full access is restored immediately.
Can I log into my account from multiple devices?
Yes! Just like Facebook, you can log in to your EduPlus account on your phone, tablet, and laptop simultaneously. Your registered courses, submissions, active days, marks, and profile details will sync automatically in real time across all devices.
How do I reset my password if I forget it?
You can reset your password by clicking "Forgot Password?" on the sign-in page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address free of charge. If you cannot remember the email, use "Find Account" to locate it and reset your password at no cost.
What should I do if I experience technical issues during a live class?
If you experience technical issues during a live class, try refreshing your browser or switching to a more stable internet connection. You can also type your answer in the Live Classroom Quick Answer section when you reconnect. For persistent issues, contact support at 0536025270.
How do I browse and join courses as a student?
Students can browse available courses using the "Browse & Join" button on their dashboard. Enter a course code to join immediately, or browse by level and semester. Once joined, course materials, assignments, and announcements will appear in your feed.
